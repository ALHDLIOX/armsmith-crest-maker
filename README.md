# Armsmith

[Armsmith](https://coatofarmsmaker.net/) is a guided AI crest design tool for families, couples, storytellers, and gaming communities.

## What it does

- Choose an occasion, visual style, colors, shield, and heraldic symbols through a four-step workflow.
- Add a name, motto, or short creative brief to shape the design.
- Generate original square crest artwork, then save, revisit, download, or reuse it from a private archive.

## Example workflow

1. Choose the occasion and overall style.
2. Select colors, shield treatment, and symbols.
3. Add a name, motto, or visual brief.
4. Generate an original crest and refine the result.

## Who it is for

Armsmith is designed for personal family projects, couples and wedding ideas, fictional houses, writers, worldbuilders, tabletop role-playing groups, and gaming communities.

## Product boundary

Armsmith creates original AI-generated designs inspired by heraldry. It does not grant official coats of arms, verify ancestry, or certify historical heraldry.
